Austin is heating up. 🌡️ Four charts from 41 years of daily weather at Camp Mabry:

1️⃣ 100°F days: about 44 a year over the last decade, up from 12 in the first. Record: 90 in 2011.
2️⃣ The first 100°F day is arriving about 11 days earlier per decade.
3️⃣ Nighttime lows are warming about 0.5°F per decade.
4️⃣ The average annual temperature is warming about 0.4°F per decade.

Data: Meteostat station 72254 (Austin Camp Mabry), through October 9, 2026.
